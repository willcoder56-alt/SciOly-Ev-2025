# Science Olympiad Electric Vehicle (2026)

CAD source for my 2026 Science Olympiad Electric Vehicle build. Full assembly built and exported from onshape

## Files

- `cad/electric_vehicle.step` — full assembly.

## Parts in the assembly

- Chassis: carbon rod x2, 125mm rod, axel holder, headpiece
- Drivetrain: 60 tooth gear, wheel (metal), wheel (plastic), wheel (rubber)
- Sensing: encoder, encoder bearing, encoder plate
- Electronics mounting: UNO holder, Battery Back, CONTROL, XD-3420, Kflo8_1, Kflo8_2
